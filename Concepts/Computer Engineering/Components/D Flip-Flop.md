A *D flip-flop*, or *Data flip-flop* is a common circuit structure used to store a single bit of data. It is very commonly used a building block in synchronous circuits.

It takes two inputs: $D$ and $CLK$. It outputs $Q$, which is the stored bit, and optionally the inverted bit $\bar Q$. On a single clock edge (usually defaulting to high), the value of $D$ is stored in the circuit.
