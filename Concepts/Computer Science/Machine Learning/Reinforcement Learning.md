## Definition
*Reinforcement learning* is a machine learning technique where the model, called an *agent*, learns what to do in an *environment* through rewards (either positive or negative) with the goal to maximize the reward. The learned behavior is called a *policy*.

## Properties / Characteristics
- **Game playing**: An agent learns to play Atari or Go by maximizing score/win rate.
- **Control systems**: A robot learns to balance a pole by receiving rewards for upright behavior.
- **Recommendation systems**: Choosing which content to show to maximize long-term engagement.
- **Resource allocation**: Dynamically adjusting pricing or inventory based on feedback.

## Examples
- Game AI
- Control systems
- Recommendation systems
