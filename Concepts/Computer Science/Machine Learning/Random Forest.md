## Definition
A *random forest* is an [[Ensemble|ensemble]] of [[Decision Tree|decision trees]] where each tree is trained on a random subset of the data and considers only a random subset of features at each split. Predictions are made by aggregating the predictions of all trees, such as voting for classification or averaging for regression. See also [[Aggregation Methods]].
