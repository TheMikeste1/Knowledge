*Deep learning* is a category of machine learning models that use deep stacks of computation. That is, instead of performing one or a few calculations, they perform many computations that feed into each other. 

Deep learning describes the *depth* of the model instead of what it's used for. As such, deep learning models can be used for all applications of machine learning, including [[Supervised Learning]], [[Unsupervised Learning]], and [[Reinforcement Learning]]. This depth allows models to excel at many traditionally difficult tasks, including image recognition, games, natural language processing, and many other categories. One of the most well-known types of deep learning is the [[Neural Network]].
