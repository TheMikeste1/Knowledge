*Overfitting* occurs when a model is too tightly tuned to its training data. This causes it to be very accurate on the training data, but perform poorly on new data. This disparity is due to the model learning the data rather than the pattern associated with the data. Overfitting is caused by
- Too much training
- The model being too complex
- Datasets that are too small
- Too many [[Feature|features]]
- Noise datasets
- Insufficient [[Regularization|regularization]]
- Extreme [[Outlier|outliers]]
- Unintentionally including information about the [[Label|label]]
