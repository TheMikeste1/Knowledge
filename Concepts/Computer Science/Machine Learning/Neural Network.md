*Neural network* (*NN*) is a subcategory of [[Deep Learning|deep learning]] models. The neural network was inspired by the architecture of a brain, consisting of one or more [[Neuron|neurons]] connected in one or more [[Layer|layers]] using [[Activation Function|activation functions]]. They use a [[Loss Function|loss function]] and an [[Optimizer|optimizer]] to adjust its neurons to minimize loss. Most optimizers are a type of [[Stochastic Gradient Descent]] or [[Mini-Batch Gradient Descent]].
