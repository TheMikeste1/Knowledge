The *Principle of Least Privilege* states that any user should have the minimum requires privileges/[[Authorization|authorization]] to do their job. The term "user" can mean anything from a human to a script, application, or automated process. A common way to handle the Principle of Least Privilege is [[Role-Based Access]].

For example, if a user's job is just to create reports, they should have read access to the data **for their report** (meaning no data they don't need) and write access to the report.
