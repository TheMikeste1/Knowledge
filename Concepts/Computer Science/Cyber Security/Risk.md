*Risk* in cybersecurity is often though of in two parts: *probability* and *impact*. Generally speaking, risk is thought of as
$$
\text{Risk} = \text{Probability} \times \text{Impact}
$$

Probability is the chance of the vulnerability being exploited.

Impact is the potential damage a vulnerability can cause.
