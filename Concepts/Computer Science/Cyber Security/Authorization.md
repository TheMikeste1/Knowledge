*Authorization* enforces rules about who can access what data and functionality.

A common misconception in authorization schemas is that an authorized *admin* user is someone who can perform any task. In reality, admin roles so be used to manager users and the application, not perform tasks associated with private data unless their management role requires it and the action does not belong to some other role.
