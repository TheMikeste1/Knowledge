A Universal Asynchronous Receiver-Transmitter, or UART, is a hardware device to transfer data. It uses electrical signals to encode bits, and is a common serial device. Data is framed then sent over the wire. There is typically a protocol layer above the UART defining what different signals mean, such as [[RS-422]].
