# What is a Multiagent System?
Goals:
- Cooperation
- Task Sharing
- Making Group Decisions
- Forming Coalitions
- Allocating resources
- Bargaining
- Arguing
## Approaches
### Centralized Control
All information is known by one decision maker who forms the matches.

### Distributed Control
Information and decisions are local. Users have access to some system to post to others or the entire group.
