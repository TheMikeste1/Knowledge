Semiconductors are materials somewhere in between [[Conductors]] and [[Insulators]]. They usually act like insulators, but can be convinced to act like conductors in certain circumstances. A well-known semiconductor is silicon.

Semiconductors are particularly useful due to their ability to act like either a conductor or insulator. The way this ability works is described by [[Quantum Mechanics|quantum mechanics]].
