There are two types of charges: positive and negative. Like charges repel, and unlike charges attract.

Good sources of charge are [[Conductors]], and poor ones are [[Insulators]].

Charge is often written as $q$ or $Q$ in equations, and is measured in *coulombs*, which is equivalent to 1 [[Current|ampere]] flowing for 1 second.

The charge of an [[Electron|electron]] $e$ is equal to $e = -1.602176565 \times 10^{-19} \text{coulomb}$.
