A function is *linear* if it has
1. Homogeneity
    - $f(ax) = af(x)$
2. Additivity
    - $f(x_1 + x_2) = f(x_1) + f(x_2)$

[[Resistor|Resistors]], [[Capacitor|capacitors]], and [[Inductor|inductors]] are all linear.

Linearity is beneficial in Electrical Engineering because it makes circuits able to be solved exactly. Non-linear circuits are much more difficult to solve, and often require mathematical adjustments to make it seem linear, at the very least over that range of operation.
