*Ohm's Law* is one of the core concepts in electrical engineering. It takes the form of a simple equation: [[voltage]] $v$ equals [[current]] $i$ times [[resistance]] $R$.
$$
v = iR
$$
Using this equation, we can solve for any of the three [[power]] components in a circuit. To solve for voltage, use the for it is already in. To solve for current,
$$
i = \frac{v}{R}
$$
And finally, to solve for resistance.
$$
R = \frac{v}{i}
$$
