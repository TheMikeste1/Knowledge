## Definition


Insulators are the opposite of [[Conductors]]. Their outer electrons are tightly bound to their nucleus and do not want to move. While it is possible to use electric forces to rip away their electrons, modest forces are only able to stretch or deform the electron cloud. If an electric force is strong enough to rip away electrons, a "breakdown" occurs. This is what happens to air molecules when a spark is seen. Glass, stone, air, and many other materials are all insulators.
