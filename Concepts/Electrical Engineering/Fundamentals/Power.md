## Definition

Power is the rate at which energy $U$ is transformed or transferred over time.[^1] Power is measured in joules/second, also known as *watts*. Power $W$ can be expressed as
$$
W = \frac{\text{d}U}{\text{d}t} = \frac{\Delta{U}}{\Delta{t}}
$$

Since $\Delta{U}$ is directly related to [[Voltage|voltage]] $V$, power can be expressed in terms of voltage and [[Current|current]] ($\frac{\Delta{q}}{\Delta{t}}$ or $i$):
$$
W
    = \frac{\Delta{U}}{\Delta{t}}
    = \frac{\Delta{U}}{\Delta{q}} \cdot \frac{\Delta{q}}{\Delta{t}}
    = V i
$$

Power is dissipated in a [[Resistor]].
