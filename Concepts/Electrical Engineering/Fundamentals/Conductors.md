## Definition


Conductors are materials whose atoms have electrons with weak bonds in the outer shell. When many of these atoms are together, atoms will share these valence electrons creating a swarm not associated with any particular nucleus. This swarm can be controlled through a small electric or magnetic force.

Some conductors, such as copper, gold, and salt water are good conductors and have a swarm that's ready to move. Others, like tungsten and diamonds, are poor conductors: they are still conductors, but their swarm resists moving more than other conductors.
