In circuit diagrams, the direction of current is shown by a small arrow next to a component labeled with an $i$. Current flows into the positive terminal of a component, which is represented by a plus next to the component. The negative terminal is additionally represented. Between the two terminals, a $v$ is placed for voltage.

Each component has a relationship to [[Voltage|voltage]] and [[Current|current]]. This is called the component's *I-V relationship*.
