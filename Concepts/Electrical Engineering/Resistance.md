Resistance $R$ is a material's opposition to the flow of electricity. It can roughly be thought of as friction, but for electricity. It is directly proportional to the [[Current|current]] flowing through it, as per [[Ohm's Law]]:
$$
v = Ri
$$

Ohm is a measure of resistance between two points. One ohm $\ohm$ is the amount of resistance in a conductor when 1 [[Voltage|volt]] is applied with a current of 1 [[Current|ampere]]:
$$
1\Omega = 1\text{volt} / \text{ampere}
$$
