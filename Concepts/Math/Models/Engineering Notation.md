Engineering notation is a way to represent particularly large or small numbers. To use engineering notation, place a decimal after the first digit and multiply it by 10 to the power of $n$ so it equals the original. $n$ will be positive for large numbers, and negative for small numbers. This $n$ is written after the decimal number after an $\text{E}$ or $\text{e}$ (meaning exponent). For example, [[Avogadro's number]] can be written as $6.02214082 \text{E}23$, and the speed of light ($299792458 m/\sec$) can be represented as $2.99792458 \text{e}8$.
