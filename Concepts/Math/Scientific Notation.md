Scientific notation is a way to represent particularly large or small numbers. To use scientific notation, place a decimal after the first digit and multiply it by 10 to the power of $n$ so it equals the original. $n$ will be positive for large numbers, and negative for small numbers. For example, [[Avogadro's number]] can be written as $6.02214082 \times 10^{23}$, and the speed of light ($299792458 m/\sec$) can be represented as $2.99792458 \times 10^{8}$.
