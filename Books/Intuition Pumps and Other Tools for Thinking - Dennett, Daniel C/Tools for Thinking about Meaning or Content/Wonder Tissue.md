## Thoughts
- "Wonder tissue" refers to magical brain tissue that can perform "miraculous" or "magical" functions [p. 99]
- When discussing or discovering technical topics, there is one rule: "No wonder tissue!"
    - The system must be explainable through understood concepts
    - By "technical," I mean "in a professional/academic/truth-seeking" context
    - This applies to religion too: no "gospel by Michael." God operates in the rules He has set forth
- Related to [[Occam's Razor]]
- If something seems too good to be true, it probably is [p. 100]
