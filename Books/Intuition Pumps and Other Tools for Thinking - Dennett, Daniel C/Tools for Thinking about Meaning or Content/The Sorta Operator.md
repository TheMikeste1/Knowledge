## Thoughts
- Agents don't always perform their function perfectly; sometimes it's just "good enough:" they "sorta" do it [p. 96]
    - This is all we need to use it as a building block/[[The Personal - Sub-personal Distinction|subpersonal part]]
- Small parts can be composed into complex things
- There's often an "evolution" from "sorta" into a "fulfilled" model
    - That is, an evolution from kinda doing it to actually doing it
    - A creature/system may begin to rely on this function
- There's not always a hard line between "sorta" and "fulfilled"
