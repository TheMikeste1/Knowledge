## Thoughts
- All of the body is part of the soul [p. 91]
    - We are spirit and body. We do not simply inhabit the mind/brain
- [[The Personal - Sub-personal Distinction|The Sub-personal parts]] can be broken down until they are so simple the can be replaced with simple "machines"
- Parts do not need to be conscious to be agents [p. 94]
- Our systems work best with healthy, but largely cooperative, competition
    - Selfish competition (e.g., cancer) destroys or transforms the original system
    - Selfish agents can still work well in a system so long as they serve some cooperative purpose in the larger whole
