## Thoughts
- Even a false belief must be supported at some point by a real belief
- Just because someone is wrong about something doesn't mean they are wrong on all accounts
- "All things testify of Christ"
    - One way or another it comes back to Him
- Can false beliefs support a true one?
    - In math: wrong equation, right solution
    - However, this is a shaky foundation
- False beliefs will either fall apart or the brain will come up with something when the belief is challenged
- We must gain an understanding to truly believe something
    - Even if it's just understanding the question
