## Definitions
**Manifest image**: How the world seems in everyday life. This include how we see things, color, culture, free will, ideas, stories, societal norms, etc. It is our interpretation of the environment we (or another [[Agent]]) lives in.

**Scientific image**: Molecules, atoms, etc. How the universe presents itself in cold, hard terms.

## Thoughts
- Manifest and Scientific image often five different answers to the same question
    - Q. Why is the sky blue?
        - A. Scientific: Rayleigh scattering
        - A. Manifest: Because air is blue
- Science can't explain everything
    - "What is the chemical composition of a bargain? What's the chemical difference between two different bargains?" [p. 70]
        - It's an absurd thing to ask.
        - Bargains, money, etc., really only exist in our minds and in meaning.
        - They are not a part of the scientific universe
- Words are how we transmit our manifest image
    - Some things simply cannot be communicated-- or understood-- without words to describe them and give them meaning
