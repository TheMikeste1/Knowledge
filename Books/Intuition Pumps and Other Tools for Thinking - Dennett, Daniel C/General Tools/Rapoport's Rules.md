## Thoughts
- Forcefully point out obvious contradictions [p. 33]
- Carefully expose somewhat hidden contradictions, then attack them [p. 33]
- Be careful searching for hidden contradictions [p. 33]
    - Don't be uncharitable in your interpretation of their work
    - Don't nitpick
    - Focus on the issue at hand (avoid semantics except where relevant)

## Quotes
> [!quote] pp. 33, 34; Summary of quote by Anatol Rapoport of [[Tit-for-Tat]] fame
> How to compose a successful critical commentary:
> 1. You should attempt to re-express your target's position so clearly, vividly, and fairly that your target says, "Thanks, I wish I'd thought of putting it that way."
> 2. You should list and points of agreement (especially if they are not matters of general or widespread agreement).
> 3. You should mention anything you have learned from your target.
> 4. Only then are you permitted to say so much as a word of rebuttal or criticism.
