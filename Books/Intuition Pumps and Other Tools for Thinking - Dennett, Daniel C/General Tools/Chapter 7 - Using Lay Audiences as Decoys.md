## Thoughts
- People tend to under-explain to experts [p. 42]
    - They expect the experts are already familiar with the topic
    - Overexplaining is often an insult
    - This can cause experts to talk past each other
- To counter: have experts present their views to audiences of non-experts with the other experts on the sideline
    - Avoids insulting experts
    - Still fully explains positions
    - Main focus should be the non-experts
        - "Interested amateurs" [p. 43] work best. Not novices– they should be somewhat familiar
