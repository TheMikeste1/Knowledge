**Abstract**:: Bryan Mealy and Fabrizio Tappero give an overview of the [[VHDL]] hardware design language and teach beginners about basic digital design.

**Notes**:: 

**Thoughts**:: Free Range VHDL is a great free resource. I think it was best understood after running through the VHDL portion of [NAND Land](https://nandland.com), after which a lot of the information is redundant but better understood due to the overview NAND Land provides. Free Range VHDL also provides some challenging exercises in the later chapters. Overall, it provides a good overview and enough to get beginners started in VHDL, though they might struggle to set up a project if they don't know what they're doing.
